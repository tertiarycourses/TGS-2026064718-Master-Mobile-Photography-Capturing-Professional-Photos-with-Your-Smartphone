# Activity 5 Guide — Complete a Composition Photo Walk

**Scenario:** A design studio needs a visual library of textures and urban details for a Singapore-themed mood board.

**Tools:** Smartphone, grid overlay, optional macro attachment

**Deliverable:** Ten-image contact sheet demonstrating ten named composition techniques

## Detailed step-by-step
1. Define a consistent theme, colour family and safe walking boundary.
2. Turn on the grid and capture one clear rule-of-thirds image.
3. Capture balance or symmetry with a level horizon and controlled edges.
4. Use negative space to isolate a small subject.
5. Use leading lines that end at a subject rather than leaving the frame.
6. Build a frame within a frame using architecture or foliage.
7. Capture pattern/texture and then break the pattern deliberately.
8. Use reflection without including an unwanted photographer image.
9. Create depth with foreground, midground and background layers.
10. Capture rule of space for a moving or looking subject, then curate and label all ten images.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Ten distinct labelled techniques
- [ ] Primary subject is immediately identifiable
- [ ] Edges are clean and horizons intentional
- [ ] No privacy or location-rule breach

## Troubleshooting

- Missing control: use the closest native mode and document the limitation.
- Soft result: clean, stabilise, re-focus and repeat.
- Clipped highlight: lower exposure or change the light/camera position.
- Unsafe/unauthorised capture: stop and use a controlled substitute.
