# Activity 6 Guide — Direct an Environmental Portrait and Property View

**Scenario:** A small-business profile needs an owner portrait plus an interior image that describes the working environment.

**Tools:** Smartphone, window light, reflector/white card, tripod if available

**Deliverable:** Three portrait variations and two corrected interior views

## Detailed step-by-step
1. Ask consent and agree how the images will be used.
2. Choose soft window light and remove distracting or confidential background objects.
3. Place the owner off-centre with looking room toward the brighter area.
4. Guide a natural action instead of a rigid pose and focus on the near eye.
5. Capture eye-level, slightly high and contextual wide portrait variations.
6. For the interior, move to a corner that shows foreground, midground and background.
7. Keep the phone level so vertical lines remain straight.
8. Use HDR where highlight/shadow contrast is high and check for ghosting.
9. Capture one balanced/symmetrical view and one leading-line view.
10. Review facial edges, verticals, reflections, privacy and the story each image tells.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Consent and use are recorded
- [ ] Portrait has credible skin tone and sharp eyes
- [ ] Interior verticals are controlled
- [ ] Five images communicate person and place

## Troubleshooting

- Missing control: use the closest native mode and document the limitation.
- Soft result: clean, stabilise, re-focus and repeat.
- Clipped highlight: lower exposure or change the light/camera position.
- Unsafe/unauthorised capture: stop and use a controlled substitute.
