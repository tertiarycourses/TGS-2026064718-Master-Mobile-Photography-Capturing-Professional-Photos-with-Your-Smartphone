# Activity 6 Checklist — Direct an Environmental Portrait and Property View

## Before capture

- [ ] Brief, audience, use and acceptance criteria understood
- [ ] Permission/consent and privacy boundary confirmed
- [ ] Lens clean; battery/storage/format checked
- [ ] Mounts, lights, remotes and cables inspected
- [ ] Safe operating area and stop condition agreed

## During capture

- [ ] Focus and important highlights checked
- [ ] Horizon, edges and background scanned
- [ ] Variation set captured rather than one frame
- [ ] Public movement and equipment stability monitored

## After capture

- [ ] Sharpness, exposure, colour and artefacts reviewed
- [ ] Required evidence saved with clear filenames
- [ ] Privacy/confidential material removed from delivery
- [ ] Acceptance criteria and peer/client feedback completed
