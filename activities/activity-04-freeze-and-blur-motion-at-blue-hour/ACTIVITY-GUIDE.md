# Activity 4 Guide — Freeze and Blur Motion at Blue Hour

**Scenario:** A city mobility campaign needs one frozen-action frame and one creative motion frame from a safe waterfront location.

**Tools:** Smartphone with burst/manual controls, tripod or stable support, Bluetooth remote

**Deliverable:** Frozen-action image, panning image, low-light comparison and settings log

## Detailed step-by-step
1. Choose a safe pedestrian viewpoint away from the travel path of moving subjects.
2. Map the subject direction and select a background with visible lines or lights.
3. For freeze: use burst or a fast shutter option and pre-focus where action will occur.
4. Capture several passes and select the frame with sharp subject and strong gesture.
5. For panning: reduce shutter speed where supported and track before releasing.
6. Continue the movement smoothly after release and repeat several passes.
7. For static low light: stabilise the phone and use night mode or a slow capture.
8. Lower exposure if bright lamps lose colour or detail.
9. Compare sharpness, noise, blur direction and highlight retention at full view.
10. Log the variables that changed the result and explain which image fits the campaign.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Capture position is safe
- [ ] Frozen frame has a readable subject
- [ ] Panning frame communicates motion
- [ ] Settings/effect relationship is explained

## Troubleshooting

- Missing control: use the closest native mode and document the limitation.
- Soft result: clean, stabilise, re-focus and repeat.
- Clipped highlight: lower exposure or change the light/camera position.
- Unsafe/unauthorised capture: stop and use a controlled substitute.
