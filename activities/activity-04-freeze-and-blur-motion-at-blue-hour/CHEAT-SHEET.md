# Activity 4 Cheat Sheet — Freeze and Blur Motion at Blue Hour

- Freeze: fast/burst · Blur: slower capture · Low light: stabilise
- Track before release and continue after release
- Safe camera position is part of the photograph's quality

## Universal capture loop

**CLEAN → STABILISE → FRAME → FOCUS → EXPOSE → CAPTURE VARIATIONS → REVIEW**

## Field reminder

The control names may vary by device. Judge the image and the evidence, not whether the menu label matches another phone.
