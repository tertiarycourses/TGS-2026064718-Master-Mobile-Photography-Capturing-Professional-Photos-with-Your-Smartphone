# Activity 2 Guide — Coordinate a Safe Retail Panorama

**Scenario:** A retail centre needs a clean panorama and supporting wide photographs showing an atrium without obstructing public movement.

**Tools:** Smartphone, tripod and clamp, Bluetooth remote, floor markers

**Deliverable:** Role call sheet, safe tripod setup, panorama and two supporting wide shots

## Detailed step-by-step
1. Walk the location and select a viewpoint with strong lines and a safe operating zone.
2. Confirm photography permission and identify faces, logos or screens to exclude.
3. Assign director, camera operator, safety observer and public liaison roles.
4. Agree the start cue, sweep direction, stop cue and interruption procedure.
5. Extend tripod legs fully on firm ground and test the clamp before mounting.
6. Set the phone at eye level, clean the lens and align a level horizon.
7. Mark a compact safety footprint without blocking exits or desire paths.
8. Run a rehearsal sweep; stop if any person enters the operating zone.
9. Capture the panorama slowly, then capture two wide stills using leading lines.
10. Inspect seams, bent geometry, moving-subject fragments and overall exposure.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Roles and stop signals are documented
- [ ] Tripod is stable and non-obstructive
- [ ] Panorama has a level horizon and clean seams
- [ ] Supporting shots meet the aesthetic brief

## Troubleshooting

- Missing control: use the closest native mode and document the limitation.
- Soft result: clean, stabilise, re-focus and repeat.
- Clipped highlight: lower exposure or change the light/camera position.
- Unsafe/unauthorised capture: stop and use a controlled substitute.
