# Activity 7 Guide — Select and Mount Specialty Camera Equipment

**Scenario:** An outdoor education provider needs a safe action sequence and asks whether a phone, action camera or creative toy-camera look is appropriate.

**Tools:** Smartphone, action camera/housing or simulator, rated mount, safety tether

**Deliverable:** Equipment decision, mounting inspection, five-shot action sequence and feedback note

## Detailed step-by-step
1. Read the action brief, environment limits and required shot sizes.
2. Compare phone, action camera and lomography-style options against the brief.
3. Select equipment based on field of view, durability, control and intended aesthetic.
4. Inspect housing seals, mount threads, clamp, tether and battery condition.
5. Choose a mounting point that does not obstruct movement, controls or protective gear.
6. Run a stationary test and apply a secondary safety tether.
7. Capture establishing, full, medium, close detail and alternative-angle shots.
8. Stop operation immediately if the mount loosens or the route becomes unsafe.
9. Review horizon, wide-angle distortion, vibration and subject visibility.
10. Write feedback recommending one change to angle, mount or movement for the next run.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Equipment choice is justified
- [ ] Mount and tether pass inspection
- [ ] Five-shot sequence covers the action
- [ ] Feedback links an observable defect to a specific correction

## Troubleshooting

- Missing control: use the closest native mode and document the limitation.
- Soft result: clean, stabilise, re-focus and repeat.
- Clipped highlight: lower exposure or change the light/camera position.
- Unsafe/unauthorised capture: stop and use a controlled substitute.
