# Activity 7 Cheat Sheet — Select and Mount Specialty Camera Equipment

- Choose gear by field of view, durability, control and aesthetic
- Inspect housing, clamp, thread, battery and tether
- Five-shot sequence: establish · full · medium · detail · alternate angle

## Universal capture loop

**CLEAN → STABILISE → FRAME → FOCUS → EXPOSE → CAPTURE VARIATIONS → REVIEW**

## Field reminder

The control names may vary by device. Judge the image and the evidence, not whether the menu label matches another phone.
