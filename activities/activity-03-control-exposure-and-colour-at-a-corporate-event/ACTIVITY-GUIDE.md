# Activity 3 Guide — Control Exposure and Colour at a Corporate Event

**Scenario:** An event organiser needs speaker and audience photographs under mixed warm stage light and cool ambient light.

**Tools:** Smartphone with Pro/Manual mode or exposure compensation, optional monopod

**Deliverable:** Exposure/focus test set, white-balance comparison and five usable event frames

## Detailed step-by-step
1. Identify the priority subject and the brightest important highlight in the scene.
2. Clean the lens, silence flash and choose a stable, permitted camera position.
3. Tap the speaker's face to focus and lower exposure until facial highlights retain detail.
4. Lock AE/AF for a consistent burst when distance and light remain unchanged.
5. Capture a short burst at a gesture or interaction, then select the sharpest frame.
6. Compare auto, warm and cool white-balance settings on the same scene.
7. Choose the setting that keeps skin credible while preserving the intended atmosphere.
8. Capture a wide establishing view, two medium interactions and two close reactions.
9. Check motion blur, focus, colour shifts, clipped screens and distracting badges.
10. Record the chosen focus, exposure and white-balance decisions in the evidence log.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Faces are sharp and readable
- [ ] Important highlights are not clipped
- [ ] White balance is consistent across the sequence
- [ ] Five frames cover place, action and reaction

## Troubleshooting

- Missing control: use the closest native mode and document the limitation.
- Soft result: clean, stabilise, re-focus and repeat.
- Clipped highlight: lower exposure or change the light/camera position.
- Unsafe/unauthorised capture: stop and use a controlled substitute.
