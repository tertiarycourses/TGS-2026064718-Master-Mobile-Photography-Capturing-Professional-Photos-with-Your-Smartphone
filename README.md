## About the course

This course covers mobile photography from camera set-up to final delivery: framing, composition, lighting, exposure and colour, then editing and enhancing photos to a professional standard. Each activity is a real use case, such as a café product shoot, a retail panorama, a corporate event or a tourism campaign.

This public learner repository contains the current slide deck, Learner Guide, Lesson Plan and eight self-contained activity packs. Detailed procedures live in the Learner Guide and the activity folders. The slide deck stays visual and concept-led.

## Learning outcomes

- **LO1.** Set up a mobile phone camera for shooting and interpret a shot plan.
- **LO2.** Perform basic mobile photography while meeting safety requirements.
- **LO3.** Apply framing and composition techniques using a mobile phone camera.
- **LO4.** Enhance visual quality and effects using capture and editing controls.

## Topics covered

1. **Setting up the mobile camera for shooting.** Types of camera and accessories, benefits of mobile photography, equipment inspection, reading and analysing a shot plan.
2. **Basic mobile photography skills.** The exposure triangle, white balance and colour temperature, manual focus vs AF lock, manual exposure vs AE lock, lens perspective and compression, safety regulations and procedures.
3. **Framing and composition.** Principles of picture composition, arranging subjects, framing with elements, leading lines, reflection, contrast, scale and depth, special effects.
4. **Enhancing visual quality and effects.** Wide, mid and close-up shots; high angle, eye level and low angle; elements of a good photograph; specialty camera equipment and accessories.
